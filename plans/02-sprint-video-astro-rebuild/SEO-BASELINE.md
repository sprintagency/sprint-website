# Sprint Video SEO baseline (captured 2026-11-12)

Captured from the live WordPress site (sprintvideo.co.uk) on 2026-11-12 by crawling `page-sitemap.xml`.
The rebuild must keep every URL, title, H1 and video below unless the plan records a deliberate change.
Raw machine-readable copy: `seo-baseline.json` in this folder.

## Site-wide facts

- 21 indexable pages, all HTTP 200, each canonical to itself.
- Host: LiteSpeed + PHP (WordPress), Elementor, ElementsKit, Essential Addons, KeyDesign framework, Debloat, Yoast SEO 27.9.
- `http://` and `www.` both 301 to `https://sprintvideo.co.uk/`.
- URLs without a trailing slash 301 to the trailing-slash form (`/about` to `/about/`).
- Unknown paths return a real 404.
- `robots.txt` allows everything and points at `/sitemap_index.xml`.
- Two other sitemaps hold Elementor plumbing that should not be indexed: `/elementskit-content/dynamic-content-widget-popup-5760fc29/`, `/?elementskit_template=header`, `/?elementskit_template=footer`.
- Schema on every page is the Yoast default only: WebSite, WebPage, Organization, BreadcrumbList, ImageObject.
- Videos: Wistia on home, white-label and example-videos; Vimeo iframes on the industry pages.
- 15 of 21 pages have no meta description, 3 have no H1, and 193 of 252 images have no alt text.
